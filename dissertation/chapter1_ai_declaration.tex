\section{Declaration Regarding Use of Generative AI}
\label{sec_intro_ai}

Generative AI models, predominantly Claude by Anthropic, were used throughout the work reported in
this dissertation. Each use is listed below, together with the level at which it was applied.

\begin{compactitem}
\item \textbf{Literature.} Finding candidate references and summarising papers.
    Every cited work was read and checked against its source before citation.
\item \textbf{Research code.} The experimental codebase was written with substantial AI assistance,
    ranging from IDE-level completion to agent-authored model variants, training and evaluation
    scripts, and experiment-tracking integration.
\item \textbf{Figures.} The data figures were produced by AI-assisted Python scripts operating on the
    logged experimental results, and the architecture and pipeline diagrams were drawn in TikZ with
    the same assistance.
\item \textbf{Text.} A first draft of every chapter was produced with AI assistance from the
    experimental record and lab book. That draft was subsequently rewritten for this document, with
    AI used to check the numerical and factual consistency of the rewritten text against the logged
    results.
\item \textbf{LaTeX and language.} Markup for tables, figures and equations, and corrections to
    grammar and formatting.
\end{compactitem}

The experimental design, the choice of experiments, and the interpretation of their results are my own.
