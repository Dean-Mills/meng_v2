\section{SA-DMoN --- Skeleton-Aware Deep Modularity Networks}
\label{sec_meth_sadmon}

A DMoN-based learned grouping head adapted for skeletons. v1 (learnable $\sigma$, clamped $\sigma$, median $\sigma$, spectral-term variants) and v2 (feature-only adjacency, entropy-only regularisation, full formulation). Ten experiments in total, all tried to fix the modularity misalignment in a different way.
