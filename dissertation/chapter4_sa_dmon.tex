\section{SA-DMoN --- Ten Experiments}
\label{sec_res_sadmon}

Full results table for all ten SA-DMoN variants (v1 with learnable / clamped / median $\sigma$, v1 with $\lambda$ sweeps, v1 without spectral term, v2 with feature-adjacency variants, v2 with entropy-type-only regularisation, v2 full). Every variant plateaus in the 0.77--0.81 synthetic range. Root cause analysis is deferred to Chapter~\ref{chap_5}, but the empirical picture is: no SA-DMoN formulation closes the gap to kNN.
