## Conflicts with our house rules

| Rule | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| (a) " - " spaced hyphen as dash in LaTeX source | **Not addressed.** No dash/hyphen rule anywhere. Elsevier retypesets at proof ("Most formatting codes will be removed and replaced on processing the article", G-2024); only observable convention is en-dash in page ranges in the reference examples. | G-2024 |
| (b) Table captions above, figure captions below | **Conflicts (partially).** G-2024: "The corresponding caption should be placed directly below the figure or table." But the two CVIU templates put table captions above (Word: "Headings should be placed above tables, left justified."; LaTeX sample: `\caption` before `tabular`). Figures-below: no conflict. Given the Guide's sentence targets the single review PDF and Elsevier retypesets, low practical risk, but the Guide's literal wording disagrees. | G-2024; TPL-DOC; TPL-TEX |
| (c) Figure fonts >= 8 pt after scaling | **No conflict.** Elsevier generic minimum is "7 pt for normal text and no smaller than 6 pt for subscript and superscript" at final printed size; 8 pt exceeds it. CVIU Guide gives no number. Note column widths 90 / 140 / 190 mm (generic) and the template's 522 pt two-column text width. | ART-SIZE; G-2024 |
| (d) Impersonal voice | **Not addressed.** No voice rule; the Guide's own example abstract in the template uses "we". | G-2024; TPL-TEX |
| (e) Numbered references from a verified BibTeX file | **No conflict at submission; note the journal style.** "References can be in any style or format as long as the style is consistent" and "The reference style used by the journal will be applied … at the proof stage". The journal's published style and the LaTeX template are author-year (`authoryear`, `model2-names.bst`, `\citep`), so a numbered submission will be converted by Elsevier at proof; to match the template exactly, compile with `authoryear` + `model2-names`. Include DOIs ("Use of DOI is highly encouraged"). | G-2024; TPL-TEX |
